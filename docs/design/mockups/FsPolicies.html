Steps

Use enquiriesOff: Enquiry is hidden from the menu and cannot be created.
Office drafts quotationsOff: Draft Quotation is hidden. Sales agents build the quotation with the customer.
Customer signature confirms the orderA signed quotation becomes a released sales order at once. No customer PO is needed.
Sales agents may add customersA new customer starts on COD with no credit limit. The office completes the record later.
